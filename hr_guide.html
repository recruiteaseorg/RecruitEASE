Platform Guide & Analytics

Maximize your recruitment workflow and view high-level platform analytics.

grid_view

1. Recruiter Workspace & Pipeline

The Workspace is your central hub for managing all applicants. It provides a real-time table of candidates applying for your active roles.

  • Search & Filter: Instantly search candidates by name, location, or filter by specific Job Titles.
  • Sort: Order candidates by application date or by their ATS Resume Score.
  • View Resume: Click the "View Resume" button to slide open a detailed profile drawer containing the candidate's parsed resume and AI evaluations.
smart_toy

2. AI ATS Resume Screening

Our system uses an advanced AI engine to automatically screen resumes against job requirements, saving hours of manual review.

  • Candidates trigger the ATS screening from their portal after uploading a resume.
  • The AI evaluates them on a scale of 0-100 based on their experience, skills, and the job description.
  • You will see the Score instantly update on the Candidate Pipeline, color-coded for quick visual triage (Green > 70, Yellow > 50, Red < 50).
swap_horiz

3. Dynamic Stage Management

Manage candidates efficiently using the real-time pipeline tracker.

  • Use the dropdown in the Stage column to move candidates between stages (Applied, Under Review, Interviewing, Offered, Rejected).
  • Automated Consideration: If you move a candidate to Considered, Yes/No buttons will appear. Clicking Yes/No will instantly fire off an automated email to the candidate informing them of their selection status!
video_camera_front

4. AI Mock Interviews

Candidates can perform asynchronous AI video/audio interviews directly from their dashboard.

  • View their AI Interview score by clicking View Resume and navigating to the AI Interview tab in the drawer.
  • The system evaluates them on Innovation, Relevancy, Accuracy, and Tone.
  • Proctoring and anti-cheating mechanisms are built-in, providing a final Integrity Score.
work

5. Job Management

Keep track of your company's open requisitions and draft new job postings.

  • Use the Post New Job tool to write descriptions or let the AI assist in generating a job description based on a title.
  • The Job Management tab provides a bird's eye view of all open roles and their respective candidate pipelines.
